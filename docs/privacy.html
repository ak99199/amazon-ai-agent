Privacy Notice – Amazon AI Agent Ads

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Amazon AI Agent Ads is an internal application used to access, analyze, and present Amazon advertising data for campaign reporting, performance analysis, keyword intelligence, and advertising optimization.

Information we process

The application may process Amazon Ads account information and advertising performance data made available through the Amazon Ads API, including advertiser profile information, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targets, search terms, impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales, orders, and related performance metrics.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide internal advertising analytics, reporting, monitoring, recommendations, and optimization support for authorized Amazon advertising accounts.

Amazon login credentials

The application does not collect or store Amazon account passwords. Authorization is handled through Login with Amazon using OAuth 2.0. Access and refresh tokens are treated as confidential credentials and are stored securely.

Sharing of information

Advertising data and authorization credentials are not sold and are not shared with unrelated third parties. Service providers may process limited data only where necessary to operate the application securely and subject to appropriate access controls.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect advertising data and credentials, including restricted access, encrypted cloud storage where applicable, and separation of application credentials from public-facing services.

Data retention

Advertising data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for reporting, historical analysis, security, troubleshooting, and legitimate business operations. Authorization credentials are removed or invalidated when access is revoked or is no longer required.

Revoking access

An authorized Amazon advertiser may revoke the application's access through the relevant Amazon account authorization settings. Once authorization is revoked, the application can no longer request new Amazon Ads API access tokens for that authorization.

Children's privacy

This application is intended for business advertising use and is not directed to children.

Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice when the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this privacy notice or the application's data practices may be directed to the administrator of the Amazon AI Agent Ads application through the business contact details associated with the corresponding Amazon Developer and Amazon Ads accounts.
